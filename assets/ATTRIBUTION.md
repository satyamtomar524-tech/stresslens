# Test portrait

`sample_face.jpg` is Google's public MediaPipe test portrait, downloaded from:
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-assets/portrait.jpg

The image depicts Barack Obama in an official White House portrait (Pete Souza).
It is used solely to test face geometry extraction. No stress or health inference
is made about the pictured person. The app's example text and rating are fictional,
independent test inputs and are not statements by or about that person.

MediaPipe model documentation:
https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker
